# Plan 05: DemoMaker Studio Roadmap & Distribution

## 1. Project Identity & Mission
**DemoMaker Studio** is a standalone, open-source automated UI demonstration and video motion graphics engine. It allows engineering and marketing teams to produce deterministic, high-fidelity walkthroughs of web and mobile apps without backend dependencies.
